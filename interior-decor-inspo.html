Interior Decor Inspo

Find ideas for your home in completed Gabs Furniture & Interiors projects. Explore room layouts, lighting, wall finishes and storage, with practical details to consider before you choose a design.

Living-room lighting

Use lamps or accent lighting near seating and the TV wall to keep one bright ceiling light from doing all the work. Note where you read, relax and watch television before choosing light positions.

Marble-effect feature walls

Use the strongest pattern on a single wall if the room already has detailed furniture. Compare samples in daylight and evening light before choosing a finish.

Fitted kitchen and island ideas

Mark the doors, drawers and appliance openings on your plan. Check that people can still pass the island while someone is cooking or opening a cabinet.

Mirrored wardrobes

A mirror on a wardrobe door can save wall space. Check where you can stand for a full-length view and what the mirror reflects during the day.
